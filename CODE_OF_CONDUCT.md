# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

Be respectful, assume good faith while still discussing evidence rigorously, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior. Harassment, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect the privacy and safety of reporters.

For a confidential conduct report, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting form and begin the title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. The maintainers will handle reports on a best-effort basis and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
